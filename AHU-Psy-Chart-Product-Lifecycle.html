Why it renews instead of declining

A traditional product's fourth stage is decline. Because AHU-Psy Chart is edge-native software with hot-reload updates and replication bundles, its fourth stage is renewal: new modules (fault detection, ML optimization, carbon reporting) reach the installed base without a hardware refresh — resetting the growth clock rather than winding down.

The loop closes on "Evolve": the same controller takes new features by hot-reload, and a proven configuration replicates onto the next unit — extending useful life and deferring obsolescence.
