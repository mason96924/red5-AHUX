Abstract

The AHU Psychrometric Diagnostic HUB turns an ordinary air handler into a self-diagnosing, self-optimizing asset. It plots the live thermodynamic state of each AHU — outside, mixed, supply, return, and every VAV zone — on an interactive psychrometric chart and computes coil energy in real time. Its defining innovation is where it runs: the full psychrometric engine, diagnostics, and interface execute on the Red5-Plus edge controller itself, as a native enteliWEB/BACnet program object.

Design & Innovation. The chart becomes a live control surface — enthalpy process vectors show the coil at work, a Givoni-tier resolver recommends strategy, and a B1–B10 climate-band engine sets adaptive supply-air targets.

Technology & Standards. BACnet-native, aligned with ASHRAE 55/62.1/90.1, with a Guideline 36-style operating-mode timeline and a resilient hot-reload plug-in runtime.

Restoration & Sustainability. Automatic economizer free-cooling and climate-adaptive supply air deliver a documented ~70–75% supply-air energy reduction versus a fixed setpoint, while edge-native operation eliminates the always-on head-end and cloud stack.

Impact & Practical Application. Operators diagnose an AHU at a glance; replication bundles make multi-unit rollout repeatable; live data integrates over MQTT, Modbus, WebSocket, and webhooks with no vendor lock-in — all in five languages.

Four Criteria

Design & Innovation

  • The psychrometric chart becomes a live control surface — OA, mixed, supply, return and every VAV zone plotted in real time.
  • Sensible / latent / total-enthalpy process vectors are drawn between states, so operators see the coil doing its work.
  • Givoni-tier resolver turns each point's position into an actionable strategy (hold, trim, cool-dehumidify, free-cool).

Technology & Standards

  • BACnet-native read/write with a built-in object-ID diagnostic that catches the top cause of silent write failures.
  • Aligned to ASHRAE 55 / 62.1 / 90.1; Guideline 36-style operating-mode timeline and supply-air reset per AHU.
  • Resilient plug-in runtime: services hot-reload with atomic rollback, self-heal, and encrypted bundle deploys.

Restoration & Sustainability

  • Automatic economizer free-cooling whenever outside-air enthalpy is below return-air enthalpy.
  • Climate-adaptive supply air (B1–B10) instead of a fixed year-round setpoint; ERV right-sizing with ROI tooling.
  • Edge-native operation removes the always-on head-end and cloud analytics stack, cutting the system's own footprint.

Impact & Practical Application

  • Diagnose an AHU at a glance — one chart with automatic optimal / comfort / warning / alarm classification.
  • Replication bundles clone a configured controller onto the next unit, making multi-AHU commissioning repeatable.
  • Live state fans out over MQTT, Modbus, WebSocket and webhooks — integrates without vendor lock-in.
What makes it unique

A full psychrometric diagnostic and climate-adaptive control stack — live OA/SA/RA/VAV plotting, enthalpy math, economizer logic and a Guideline 36-style mode timeline — delivered directly on a BACnet edge controller in the browser, in five languages, with zero cloud dependency.
