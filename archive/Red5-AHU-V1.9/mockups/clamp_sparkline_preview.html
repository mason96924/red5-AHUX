Clamp-effectiveness sparkline

Inline 50px sparkline next to the "Live: 45-55% RH" chip in the side panel. Plots the mean SA-RH across all AHUs over the last hour (12 samples @ 5-min cadence) against the operator's clamp window. Tells the operator "is the equipment honoring the target?" at a glance.

SVG content (no PNG): 60-min window with the operator band drawn as a dim emerald rectangle, sparkline in saturated emerald, endpoint dot, numeric mean to the right. Tooltip on hover shows per-AHU breakdown + honored/total count.

Border turns rose, sparkline turns rose, numeric mean gets an up-arrow, an inline "!" glyph appears at the right edge of the SVG. Tooltip gives the per-AHU "honored/total" breakdown so the operator can isolate the misbehaving AHU.
State C — no clamp applied (factory bands — sparkline still shown as a baseline)
Live: factory bands
64%
Greyed sparkline, no band rectangle (no clamp to evaluate against), endpoint dot in slate. Still useful as a "what is my SA-RH actually doing right now?" baseline before the operator decides whether to clamp.
