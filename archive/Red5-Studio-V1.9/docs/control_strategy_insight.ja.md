## <!--FROZEN-->TL<!--/FROZEN-->;<!--FROZEN-->DR<!--/FROZEN-->

## 1. The math behind the surprise

The fixed-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> "Total" curve on the T×Time chart integrates:

```
cumTotal[i] = Σ |h_oa[i] − h_sa_user|
```

When <!--FROZEN-->`h_sa_user ≈ mean(h_oa)`<!--/FROZEN--> over the window, this integral becomes a
centered absolute deviation — and **it can shrink to a small fraction of
its maximum**.  In the extreme: if every hour had <!--FROZEN-->`h_oa = h_sa_user`<!--/FROZEN-->,
Total → 0.

By contrast, <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN--> has a **structural floor**:

```
cumB[i] = Σ |OAD/100 × (h_oa[i] − h_sa_band[i])|
```

Even at 15 % minimum <!--FROZEN-->OA<!--/FROZEN--> damper (the legal ventilation minimum), the <!--FROZEN-->AHU<!--/FROZEN-->
still conditions 15 % of <!--FROZEN-->OA<!--/FROZEN--> flow from outside enthalpy to the band's <!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN-->
enthalpy.  That term is non-zero on every hour of every season — there
is no "perfect" <!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> the strategy can settle on because each band has its
own <!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> target.

**Result:** in a stable, near-comfort week, fixed-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN-->'s centered
deviation can dip *below* <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN-->'s irreducible 15 %-<!--FROZEN-->OA<!--/FROZEN--> conditioning
floor.

## 2. Five reasons that "win" doesn't translate to operations

| # | Constraint                          | Why it kills the fixed-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> win |
|---|-------------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| 1 | **Foresight required**              | The optimal <!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> can only be chosen *after* you've seen the week's weather.  Real <!--FROZEN-->BMS<!--/FROZEN--> controllers can't time-travel. |
| 2 | **Multi-zone reality**              | One <!--FROZEN-->AHU<!--/FROZEN--> feeds many VAVs.  Perimeter zones (solar gain) and core zones (occupant heat) have different demands at the same instant.  Fixed <!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> satisfies neither well. |
| 3 | **Coil work ≠ enthalpy delta**      | The chart shows |Δh|. Fixed-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> hides this by integrating air-side enthalpy only. |
| 4 | **Ventilation code minimums**       | Hospitals, labs, and most non-residential spaces require ≥ 15 % <!--FROZEN-->OA<!--/FROZEN--> at all times for <!--FROZEN-->IAQ<!--/FROZEN-->.  <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN--> honors this. ## 3. The right way to read the chart

The fixed-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> "Total" curve is a **theoretical lower bound** of what an
idealized, omniscient <!--FROZEN-->AHU<!--/FROZEN--> could spend on a single zone with no humidity
constraint.  It is a useful *reference floor*, not an achievable
operating point.

<!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN-->'s curve is what you **actually spend** under realistic operating
conditions, including:

- Real-time controllability (no future-peek)
- Multi-zone delivery with reheat
- Latent management decoupled from sensible
- Equipment protection minimums (heating-coil freeze, dehumidification
  reheat avoidance)
- <!--FROZEN-->ASHRAE<!--/FROZEN--> 62.1 <!--FROZEN-->OA<!--/FROZEN--> minimums

When <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN--> sits above an aggressively-tuned fixed-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> in a short window,
the message is:

> "If you hand-tuned this exact week with perfect foresight, you could
>  match what <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN--> does automatically across *any* week of *any*
>  climate."

That's not an indictment of <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN-->.  That's an indictment of the
hypothetical that fixed-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> control can ever be hand-tuned that well, in
practice, across all 52 weeks of a calendar year, in every zone of a
hospital.

## 4. The Year-window proof

Re-run the same comparison with **Duration = Year** instead of Week:

- The fixed-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> Total balloons because the user-chosen <!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> can't be
  optimal for *both* January and July
- <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN--> stays flat because it auto-shifts setpoints per band
- The ratio settles at 60-80 % savings — the durable,
  climate-agnostic figure on the Monthly × Sites view

This is the figure to quote in commissioning reports, energy audits, and
<!--FROZEN-->ESG<!--/FROZEN--> disclosures.  Short-window optima are interesting for diagnostic
intuition but **never** for operating decisions.

## 5b. Dynamic Reset (<!--FROZEN-->ASHRAE<!--/FROZEN--> Guideline 36) — the real-world competitor

Naked fixed-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> control is a strawman.  The actual modern competitor to
<!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN--> is **Dynamic Reset / Trim & Respond**, codified in <!--FROZEN-->ASHRAE<!--/FROZEN-->
Guideline 36.  Instead of a single static <!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> setpoint, the <!--FROZEN-->AHU<!--/FROZEN-->
re-targets its <!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> every few minutes based on what the served zones are
actually demanding:

```
zones reporting "too hot"  → trim SA down by 0.1 °C every 2 min
zones reporting "too cold" → respond SA up by 0.2 °C
within deadband            → hold
```

Same logic also resets:
- <!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> static pressure (fan-kW savings)
- Chilled water supply temperature (chiller <!--FROZEN-->COP<!--/FROZEN-->)
- Hot water supply temperature (boiler condensing economy)

### How Dynamic Reset compares to <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN-->

| Aspect                       | Dynamic Reset (<!--FROZEN-->G36<!--/FROZEN-->)                | <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN-->                         |
|------------------------------|-------------------------------------|---------------------------------|
| Foresight needed             | No (reacts to zone demand)          | No (reacts to <!--FROZEN-->OA<!--/FROZEN--> condition)    |
| Multi-zone aware             | ✅ Yes (the whole point)            | ⚠️ Partial (<!--FROZEN-->VAV<!--/FROZEN--> reheat handles) |
| Latent control               | Add-on (<!--FROZEN-->DOAS<!--/FROZEN-->, dewpoint reset)       | ✅ Built-in (<!--FROZEN-->B7<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN--> = dehumid)  |
| Implementation complexity    | High (zone request infrastructure)  | Low (flat lookup table)        |
| Hardware needed              | Per-zone controllers + <!--FROZEN-->BMS<!--/FROZEN--> network  | Just <!--FROZEN-->OA<!--/FROZEN--> T/<!--FROZEN-->RH<!--/FROZEN--> sensor            |
| Failure mode                 | Stale request → drift               | <!--FROZEN-->OA<!--/FROZEN--> fail → fallback to <!--FROZEN-->B5<!--/FROZEN-->/<!--FROZEN-->B6<!--/FROZEN-->    |
| Energy savings vs fixed-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN-->   | 30–55 % (<!--FROZEN-->ASHRAE<!--/FROZEN--> figures)            | 60–80 % (chart data)           |
| Energy savings vs each other | (baseline)                          | ~10–25 % additional            |

### Where Dynamic Reset wins

- **Buildings with highly variable internal loads** (offices with
  shifting occupancy, conference centers): zone-demand feedback catches
  things <!--FROZEN-->OA<!--/FROZEN-->-based logic can't see.
- **Tightly tuned existing <!--FROZEN-->BMS<!--/FROZEN--> systems**: <!--FROZEN-->G36<!--/FROZEN--> sequences integrate well
  with major <!--FROZEN-->BMS<!--/FROZEN--> platforms (Tridium, Schneider <!--FROZEN-->EBO<!--/FROZEN-->, Siemens Desigo).
- **Spec-driven new construction**: <!--FROZEN-->ASHRAE<!--/FROZEN--> 36 is the de facto <!--FROZEN-->US<!--/FROZEN-->/<!--FROZEN-->AU<!--/FROZEN-->
  standard.  <!--FROZEN-->LEED<!--/FROZEN--> and <!--FROZEN-->NABERS<!--/FROZEN--> often expect it.

### Where <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN--> wins

- **Climate-driven envelope-dominated buildings** (hospitals, museums,
  schools): the dominant load is <!--FROZEN-->OA<!--/FROZEN--> enthalpy.  <!--FROZEN-->OA<!--/FROZEN-->-anchored bands track
  it more directly than zone-demand summing.
- **Edge controllers without <!--FROZEN-->BMS<!--/FROZEN--> integration**: <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN--> runs from a
  single <!--FROZEN-->OA<!--/FROZEN--> sensor.  No zone-demand network required.
- **Latent-heavy climates** (Singapore, Manila, Taipei, Houston): the
  band table explicitly hands off to dehumidification <!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> targets
  (95 % <!--FROZEN-->RH<!--/FROZEN-->, 11–13 °C) at the right thresholds.  <!--FROZEN-->G36<!--/FROZEN--> needs a separate
  dewpoint-reset rule on top.
- **Resilience**: a single misbehaving zone controller can drag a <!--FROZEN-->G36<!--/FROZEN-->
  system into wasteful trimming.  <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN--> is immune — its input is the
  weather, not the zones.

### How the chart estimates Dynamic Reset

The <!--FROZEN-->`Dyn-Rst`<!--/FROZEN--> solid purple line uses a defensible textbook model:

This captures <!--FROZEN-->G36<!--/FROZEN-->'s aggregate behaviour: <!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> tracks recent <!--FROZEN-->OA<!--/FROZEN--> conditions

<!--FROZEN-->`Σ |h_oa[i] − rolling_mean[i]|`<!--/FROZEN--> — formally, an exponentially-decaying

### The pragmatic synthesis

The two strategies aren't actually mutually exclusive.  The strongest
real-world setup combines them:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│  B1-B10 picks the band based    │
│  on OA T/RH every 5 minutes     │  ← climate-driven primary control
└─────────────┬───────────────────┘
              │ provides SA setpoint envelope
              ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│  Dynamic Reset trims within     │
│  ±2 °C of band's SA based on    │  ← zone-driven fine tuning
│  zone-demand requests           │
└─────────────────────────────────┘
```

Combined behaviour:

- <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN--> ensures you're **never operating in the wrong regime** — no
  cooling when you should be dehumidifying, no 100 % <!--FROZEN-->OA<!--/FROZEN--> when h_oa > h_sa
- Dynamic Reset captures the **last 5–10 % of zone-specific savings**
  without risking regime mismatch
- The combined system is **failsafe**: zone telemetry loss → falls back
  to pure <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN--> instead of stuck-at-default fixed-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN-->

This hybrid approach is the recommended commissioning posture for new
hospital projects using a <!--FROZEN-->Red5 Studio<!--/FROZEN--> controller alongside an existing
<!--FROZEN-->G36<!--/FROZEN-->-compliant <!--FROZEN-->BMS<!--/FROZEN-->.

## 6. Talking points for skeptical engineers

> "Why would <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN--> not always be lower?"

Because in a short, stable window the centered absolute deviation of <!--FROZEN-->OA<!--/FROZEN-->
enthalpy around its mean can dip below <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN-->'s 15 %-<!--FROZEN-->OA<!--/FROZEN--> conditioning
floor.  This is a mathematical edge case, not a control-strategy
weakness.

> "Then why don't we just hand-tune <!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> per week?"

Because (a) you'd need to know each week's mean enthalpy in advance, (b)
the optimal <!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> is different for each <!--FROZEN-->AHU<!--/FROZEN-->/zone/floor, and (c) the
short-window optimum loses to <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN--> over a full year by 60-80 % — see
the Monthly × Sites comparison.

> "Couldn't an <!--FROZEN-->AI<!--/FROZEN--> predict the optimal weekly <!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN-->?"

Yes, but the <!--FROZEN-->AI<!--/FROZEN--> would converge on something that looks remarkably like
<!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN-->: ten control regimes, each tuned to a region of the
psychrometric chart.  We've encoded that knowledge as a static table so
the controller doesn't need an <!--FROZEN-->ML<!--/FROZEN--> model to operate.
