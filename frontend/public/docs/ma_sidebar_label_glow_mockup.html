Needs column — what each category requires

“Needs” is the minimum sensor / status set to confirm that category (not just raise the glow). Without it, several causes look the same on the chart.

A
Off-chord + small damper_mismatch

Points to: Sensor / probe (MAT or MAH)

Needs: MAT + MAH, OAD

Why those needs: MAT alone cannot prove off-chord (W is forced onto the line). You need MAH for independent humidity, and OAD to show the mix ratio still matches the damper — so the fault is the sensor, not economizer.

B
Off-chord + large damper_mismatch

Points to: Economizer / damper / leakage / stratification

Needs: MAT, OAD (± MAH)

Why those needs: Compare temperature-derived OA fraction from MAT to commanded OAD. Large disagreement ⇒ damper/leak/stratification. MAH is optional: it strengthens the case but is not required to flag a damper mismatch.

C
mat_outside_oa_ra (f < 0 or > 1)

Points to: Sensor bias or stratification

Needs: MAT, OA, RA, ΔT ≥ ~2 °C

Why those needs: Fraction f = (MAT−RA)/(OA−RA) is junk when OA≈RA. You need a usable ΔT between OA and RA (~2 °C APAR floor) plus all three temperatures so “MAT outside the OA–RA range” is a real APAR-style rule, not noise.

D
Off-chord wetter; OAD ≈ minimum

Points to: Unmeasured moisture / EA path / duct leak

Needs: MAT + MAH

Why those needs: Extra water mass only shows when MA humidity is measured (MAT+MAH). Without MAH you cannot see “wetter than the chord.” OAD≈min is the operating context, not an extra sensor.

E
Plotted OA ≠ air entering mix box

Points to: ERV / HRV

Needs: EA T/RH, wheel enable, or OA-after-ERV

Why those needs: Free-stream OA on the chart is not the OA after the wheel. You need wheel enable and either exhaust air or OA after ERV to show the transfer — otherwise ERV looks identical to a bad OA/MA sensor.

F
Off-chord only when wheel ON

Points to: ERV / HRV (confirmed)

Needs: Wheel status + same MA/OA/RA sensors

Why those needs: Controlled A/B: deviation appears with wheel ON and clears with wheel OFF, using the same sensors. Wheel status is mandatory; no new points beyond what already showed the off-chord.

G
Off-chord mainly at high OAD

Points to: Economizer leakage / nonlinear damper / bypass

Needs: OAD + MAT (± MAH)

Why those needs: Correlate deviation with OAD position over time. MAT tracks whether mix quality collapses at extreme economizer open. MAH optional for off-chord confirmation at those positions.

H
Persistent bias vs OAD & wheel

Points to: Sensor drift (OA, RA, or MA)

Needs: Cross-check / handheld

Why those needs: Process signals (OAD, wheel) do not explain the bias. Confirm with a second sensor or handheld on OA, RA, or MA — the BMS points alone cannot tell which of the three drifted.
