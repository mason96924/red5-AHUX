# Opt-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> Insight

> A plain-language guide to the **Opt-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN-->** (Optimal Supply Air) strategy in the
> <!--FROZEN-->Red5<!--/FROZEN--> Building Diagnostic Command Center — what it does, how it compares to
> <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->–<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN--> (Givoni bands) and Dyn-Reset, and how to read the envelope-sliders
> + floor-warning chip without getting lost in psychrometrics.

---

## 1. The mental model — Opt-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> is an *adjustable fence*

Think of every 1 kg "puff" of air as carrying a backpack of two things:

1. **Heat** — how hot it is. 🔥
2. **Moisture** — how much water is dissolved in it. 💧

**Enthalpy <!--FROZEN-->`h`<!--/FROZEN--> (kJ/kg)** is the total weight of that backpack. Hotter AND
wetter air ⇒ heavier backpack.

Opt-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> sets a "comfort fence" in backpack-weight units:

```
   ┌──────── max (upper fence) ─────────┐
   │                                    │
   │   "happy zone" — do nothing here   │
   │                                    │
   └──────── min (lower fence) ─────────┘
```

For every hour the controller does the cheapest thing possible:

| Condition | What Opt-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> does | Energy cost |
|---|---|---|
| <!--FROZEN-->OA<!--/FROZEN--> backpack heavier than upper fence | Cool <!--FROZEN-->DOWN<!--/FROZEN--> to the fence, then stop | proportional to <!--FROZEN-->`h_oa − max`<!--/FROZEN--> |
| <!--FROZEN-->OA<!--/FROZEN--> backpack lighter than lower fence | Heat <!--FROZEN-->UP<!--/FROZEN--> to the fence, then stop | proportional to <!--FROZEN-->`min − h_oa`<!--/FROZEN--> |
| <!--FROZEN-->OA<!--/FROZEN--> already inside the fence | Don't touch the air | **0** — free! |

That's the whole strategy. It's the **bare minimum** the building could ever
do while still keeping conditioned air inside an acceptable comfort band.
That's why Opt-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> is plotted as the **theoretical energy floor** on the
Monthly × Sites chart — *no real-world strategy can do less work and still
deliver comfort*.

## 2. How Opt-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> compares to the other strategies

| Strategy | What it picks for <!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> each hour | How aggressive |
|---|---|---|
| **Fixed-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN-->** | A constant <!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> enthalpy the operator sets (e.g. 38 kJ/kg) | One-size-fits-all — wasteful in mild weather, undersized in extremes |
| **<!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN--> (Givoni bands)** | One of 10 fixed <!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> targets based on <!--FROZEN-->OA<!--/FROZEN--> temperature & humidity | A discrete "menu" of 10 backpacks (25.8 → 52.9 kJ/kg) |
| **Dyn-Reset** | A 24-h rolling mean of <!--FROZEN-->`h_oa`<!--/FROZEN-->, clamped to the Opt-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> envelope | Drifts smoothly with weather; can lag heat waves |
| **<!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN--> + Dyn-Reset** | Band <!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> target ± 5 kJ/kg, biased by Dyn-Reset | Hybrid: band's structure + Dyn-Reset's responsiveness |
| **Opt-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN-->** | <!--FROZEN-->`clamp(h_oa, min, max)`<!--/FROZEN--> — closest fence to current <!--FROZEN-->OA<!--/FROZEN--> | Cheapest possible, by definition |

Visual mental map of typical cumulative energy curves over a year:

```
Energy ▲
  ($)  │                         Fixed-SA  ━━━━━━━━━━━━━
       │                        B1-B10    ━━━━━━━━━━━━
       │                        Band+Dyn  ━━━━━━━━━━━
       │                        Dyn-Reset ━━━━━━━━━━
       │                        Opt-SA    ━━━━━━━━━━   ◀ floor
       └─────────────────────────────────────────────► Time
```

If your Opt-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> line ever climbs *above* one of the others, something is off —
either the envelope is too tight (see §5) or the band table is mistuned.

## 3. Why Opt-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> "snaps onto" <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN--> when you raise <!--FROZEN-->`max`<!--/FROZEN-->

<!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN-->'s <!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> targets span roughly **25.8 → 52.9 kJ/kg** (<!--FROZEN-->B9<!--/FROZEN--> dry & cool → <!--FROZEN-->B6<!--/FROZEN-->
warm & humid). When you slide the <!--FROZEN-->`max`<!--/FROZEN--> knob:

| <!--FROZEN-->`max`<!--/FROZEN--> setting | What Opt-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> does in summer | Energy curve behavior |
|---|---|---|
| **80 kJ/kg** (way above bands) | Almost never has to cool — fence too far away | Opt-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> flat, **far below** <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN--> (big gap) |
| **52.9** (= <!--FROZEN-->B6<!--/FROZEN-->'s <!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN-->) | Cools to ~53; <!--FROZEN-->B6<!--/FROZEN--> also targets ~53 | **Touches / overlaps** <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN--> during <!--FROZEN-->B6<!--/FROZEN--> hours |
| **30** (= <!--FROZEN-->B7<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B8<!--/FROZEN-->'s <!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN-->) | Cools all the way to 30, even when <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN--> would only cool to 50 | Opt-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> **rises**, may match or exceed <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN--> |

**Why the overlap?** As you bring <!--FROZEN-->`max`<!--/FROZEN--> toward one of the band <!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> targets,
the two strategies start picking the *same* <!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> setpoint for the same hour →
same conditioning work → energy curves trace each other.

This is also why a *very tight* envelope can make Opt-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> *more aggressive*
than <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN--> in mild weather: the fence forces aggressive cooling in hours
where some bands would happily deliver warmer <!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN-->. **That breaks the floor
property.** See §5.

## 4. What 25.8 and 52.9 kJ/kg actually mean

These are the lightest and heaviest "air backpacks" your <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN--> system would
ever ask the <!--FROZEN-->AHU<!--/FROZEN--> to deliver into the zone:

| Band | <!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> target (T, <!--FROZEN-->RH<!--/FROZEN-->) | Backpack weight |
|---|---|---|
| **<!--FROZEN-->B9<!--/FROZEN-->** | 15 °C, 40 % <!--FROZEN-->RH<!--/FROZEN--> (cool & dry) | **25.8 kJ/kg** — lightest |
| **<!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->** | 21 °C, 40 % <!--FROZEN-->RH<!--/FROZEN--> | 36.8 kJ/kg |
| **<!--FROZEN-->B7<!--/FROZEN-->** | 12 °C, 95 % <!--FROZEN-->RH<!--/FROZEN--> | 33.0 kJ/kg |
| **<!--FROZEN-->B8<!--/FROZEN-->** | 13 °C, 95 % <!--FROZEN-->RH<!--/FROZEN--> | 35.5 kJ/kg |
| **<!--FROZEN-->B5<!--/FROZEN-->** | 23.5 °C, 50 % <!--FROZEN-->RH<!--/FROZEN--> | 46.6 kJ/kg |
| **<!--FROZEN-->B6<!--/FROZEN-->** | 25 °C, 55 % <!--FROZEN-->RH<!--/FROZEN--> (warm, mildly humid) | **52.9 kJ/kg** — heaviest |

Computed live by the dashboard from the <!--FROZEN-->BANDS<!--/FROZEN--> table in <!--FROZEN-->`psy-3d-engine.js`<!--/FROZEN-->
(line ~348). Retune <!--FROZEN-->`sa_t`<!--/FROZEN--> / <!--FROZEN-->`sa_rh`<!--/FROZEN--> and these bounds follow automatically.

### What does the delta (52.9 − 25.8 = **27 kJ/kg**) mean?

For one kilogram of air, **27 kJ** of energy is roughly enough to do <!--FROZEN-->EITHER<!--/FROZEN-->:

- 🌡️ **Pure sensible cooling:** drop the air temperature by **~27 °C** without
  changing its moisture content
- 💧 **Pure latent drying:** remove **~11 grams of water** from each kilogram
  of air (about a teaspoon and a half) at constant temperature
- 🌗 **A typical real mix:** cool by ~15 °C *and* remove ~5 g of water

### Scaling to a real <!--FROZEN-->AHU<!--/FROZEN-->

A typical commercial <!--FROZEN-->AHU<!--/FROZEN--> pushes around **5 000 L/s ≈ 6 kg/s** of air.

```
27 kJ/kg × 6 kg/s = 162 kW
```

So the 27 kJ/kg menu spread translates to roughly **162 kW of
conditioning-load swing** — the <!--FROZEN-->AHU<!--/FROZEN--> might be lazily delivering ~30 kW on a
mild day (<!--FROZEN-->B6<!--/FROZEN--> light backpack) or hammering at ~190 kW on a peak monsoon day
(<!--FROZEN-->B9<!--/FROZEN--> / <!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN--> heavy lift). That's a meaningful chunk of your annual electricity
bill, and it's exactly the spread Opt-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> is trying to minimise.

## 5. The "NOT A <!--FROZEN-->TRUE<!--/FROZEN--> <!--FROZEN-->FLOOR<!--/FROZEN-->" warning chip

The amber chip beneath the Opt-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> envelope sliders fires whenever your
fence shrinks **inside** the <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN--> <!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN-->-enthalpy range (25.8 – 52.9 kJ/kg).
Two failure modes it warns about:

| Condition | What it means physically |
|---|---|
| <!--FROZEN-->`min > 25.8`<!--/FROZEN--> | Opt-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> is forced <!--FROZEN-->UP<!--/FROZEN--> to a fence that's heavier than <!--FROZEN-->B9<!--/FROZEN-->'s target. In dry-mild weather, <!--FROZEN-->B9<!--/FROZEN--> cools the air to 25.8 but Opt-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> refuses to go below <!--FROZEN-->`min`<!--/FROZEN--> → Opt-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> does **more** latent work than <!--FROZEN-->B9<!--/FROZEN--> → curve rises **above** <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN-->. |
| <!--FROZEN-->`max < 52.9`<!--/FROZEN--> | Opt-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> is forced <!--FROZEN-->DOWN<!--/FROZEN--> to a fence that's lighter than <!--FROZEN-->B6<!--/FROZEN-->'s target. In warm-humid weather, <!--FROZEN-->B6<!--/FROZEN--> only cools to 52.9 but Opt-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> cools to <!--FROZEN-->`max`<!--/FROZEN--> → Opt-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> does **more** sensible work than <!--FROZEN-->B6<!--/FROZEN--> → curve rises **above** <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN-->. |

When the warning fires, Opt-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> **stops being a theoretical floor** — it
becomes a "what-if we forced strict comfort limits" scenario. Still useful
for regulated environments (labs, pharma cleanrooms, museums) where you
*want* a tighter envelope than <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN--> provides, but it's no longer the
mathematical lower bound on energy.

### Rule of thumb

| Goal | Suggested <!--FROZEN-->`[min, max]`<!--/FROZEN--> | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Opt-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> stays a true floor** | <!--FROZEN-->`min ≤ 25.8`<!--/FROZEN-->, <!--FROZEN-->`max ≥ 53`<!--/FROZEN--> | Fully encloses <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN-->'s <!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> range; warning silent |
| **Tight commercial comfort** | <!--FROZEN-->`min = 30`<!--/FROZEN-->, <!--FROZEN-->`max = 45`<!--/FROZEN--> | Triggers warning, but reflects <!--FROZEN-->ASHRAE<!--/FROZEN--> 55 acceptable indoor envelope |
| **Lab / pharma (regulated)** | <!--FROZEN-->`min = 35`<!--/FROZEN-->, <!--FROZEN-->`max = 40`<!--/FROZEN--> | Triggers warning (intentional — you accept higher energy for stricter comfort) |

### Recovery
- Want the warning gone? Slide <!--FROZEN-->`min`<!--/FROZEN--> down to 25 or less, <!--FROZEN-->`max`<!--/FROZEN--> up to 55 or
  more. The chip vanishes silently.
- Want to keep your tighter envelope? Ignore the chip — it's informational,
  not blocking. Just read the Opt-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> line as "your custom comfort floor"
  rather than "absolute energy floor".

## 6. Reading the Monthly × Sites chart in one minute

1. **Top toolbar (Strategy dropdown)** — pick which strategies overlay
   (Band, Band+Dyn, Dyn, Opt, Fixed, $-violation).
2. **Mode chips (A / B / C / $)** down the right edge of the toolbar:
   - **A** = comfort hours covered per strategy (latent coverage)
   - **B** = energy cost per strategy ($/year, uses live tariff config)
   - **C** = energy intensity (kWh/m² · yr — apples-to-apples vs. <!--FROZEN-->ASHRAE<!--/FROZEN-->)
   - **$** = violation cost (uncovered humid hours × $/hr rate)
3. **Opt-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> envelope card** — purple-bordered popover; drag the two
   sliders to retune the fence. Floor warning lives at the bottom.
4. **Curves** — annual cumulative energy/cost. Read top-to-bottom: most
   wasteful on top, theoretical floor at bottom.

A healthy reading looks like:

```
Fixed-SA  ▔▔▔▔▔▔▔▔▔  (highest — wasteful baseline)
 │                  ↓ ~20-30% savings
B1-B10    ━━━━━━━━━  (clean band-based)
 │                  ↓ ~5-10% extra
Band+Dyn  ──────────  (hybrid)
 │                  ↓ ~5-10% extra
Dyn-Reset ──────────  (continuous reset)
 │                  ↓ small final gap
Opt-SA    ──────────  (theoretical floor)
```

If the gaps look wrong (e.g. Dyn-Reset above <!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN-->, or Opt-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> above
anything), check the envelope warning first — that's the #1 cause.

## 7. Cheat sheet — three rules to remember

1. **Opt-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> is a fence, not a fixed target.** Wider fence = less work =
   lower energy. Narrower fence = more work = higher energy.
2. **<!--FROZEN-->B1<!--/FROZEN-->-<!--FROZEN-->B10<!--/FROZEN-->'s <!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> targets span 25.8 → 52.9 kJ/kg.** Keep your fence
   *outside* that range for Opt-<!--FROZEN-->SA<!--/FROZEN--> to remain a true energy floor.
3. **The 27 kJ/kg delta ≈ 162 kW** of <!--FROZEN-->AHU<!--/FROZEN--> conditioning swing for a typical
   ~6 kg/s unit. That's why band-tuning is worth doing well — every
   percentage point of strategy improvement on this strip translates to
   real kilowatts and real money.
