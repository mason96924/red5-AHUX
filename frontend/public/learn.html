A · Comfort

Sweet spot. Inside the comfort zone and in the 40–60% RH band. Hold the setpoints — nothing to do.

B · Soft Trim

Temperature is fine, humidity is drifting. A light humidify or dehumidify — no temperature change needed.

C+H · Hot / Humid

Warm air carrying lots of moisture (think summer in Houston). Cool + dehumidify.

C+D · Hot / Dry

Warm and arid (think Phoenix in July). Cool + humidify — evaporative cooling shines here.

C−H · Cool / Wet

Cool but clammy (shoulder seasons, basements, marine climates). Heat + dehumidify.

C−D · Cool / Dry

Below 23.5 °C with low absolute moisture — typical of heated indoor air in winter. Heat + humidify if RH drops below ~30%.
