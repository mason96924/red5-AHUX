T-CLIPPED RH-BAND — 3D WX MOCKUP (iter 3)

Compares the current production RH-band slab (spans T = -15..50 °C, the full chart) against a T-clipped variant bounded to the occupied-space comfort range (default 21..27 °C, ASHRAE 55). The clipped slab focuses the operator's attention on the only region where RH control is actionable — at outdoor air below 5 °C the saturation envelope makes the band a near-zero-thickness ribbon anyway, so we lose nothing useful and gain a much cleaner read of the climate scatter. Drag the T MIN / T MAX sliders below to feel out a different clip range.

How the production engine would change:
Geometry — in _buildRhBandSlab(rhLo, rhHi), replace the for (i = 0; i <= nT; i++) { T = T_MIN + (T_MAX - T_MIN) * (i / nT); ... } loop with one that walks T = T_CLIP_LO .. T_CLIP_HI. All other math is unchanged — same two-curve sampling, same 4-face extruded mesh, same outlines.

Two new constants: RH_BAND_T_CLIP_LO = 21 and RH_BAND_T_CLIP_HI = 27 at the top of psy-3d-engine.js. We could surface them as a sub-control in the sidebar later, but starting with hard-coded ASHRAE-55 limits keeps the new feature free of extra UI.

In-band scatter highlight — behaviour unchanged. Highlighting based on p.rh ∈ [rhLo, rhHi] alone keeps the markers honest: every reading that's actually in the RH window gets the 1.6× treatment regardless of T. The T clip only adjusts the SLAB volume. This way the visual cue "look how often the air sits in my RH window" still works for the entire dataset; it's just the slab geometry that says "this is the controllable region".

Optional — if you also want the highlighted scatter to honour the T clip (so only samples inside the slab itself get popped), add p.t >= T_CLIP_LO && p.t <= T_CLIP_HI to the inFlag predicate. Say the word and I'll swap it.
